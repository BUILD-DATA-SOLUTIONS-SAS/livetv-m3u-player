This Privacy Policy explains what information Live TV: M3U Player (the "App") collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. About the App

Live TV: M3U Player is a media player. It plays IPTV / M3U playlists and streams that you add yourself, either by entering a playlist URL or by opening a playlist file on your device. The App does not provide, host, sell or distribute any channels, playlists or media content.

The App does not require an account. You do not sign up, log in, or provide your name or email address to use it.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following data is created by your use of the App and is kept locally on your device (in the App's private storage). It is not uploaded to our servers, and we do not have access to it:

  • Playlists you add (names, URLs or file references) and the channel lists parsed from them
  • Your favorites and viewing history
  • App settings and preferences (for example language)

Removing this data is under your control: delete playlists or history inside the App, clear the App's storage in Android Settings, or uninstall the App. The App does not use cloud backup (Android backup is disabled for the App).

When you play a playlist or stream, the App connects directly from your device to the playlist URL or stream server that you specified. Those servers are operated by third parties, and they may receive your IP address and standard request information, as with any media player or web browser. We do not control those servers and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

4. Advertising ID and ad-related permissions

The App declares the following advertising-related permissions: com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID (access to the Android advertising ID), ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION (access to the Android Privacy Sandbox ad-services APIs for ad ID and attribution). The advertising ID is a resettable identifier that advertising and attribution partners use to deliver ads, limit how often you see an ad, and measure ad performance.

You can control the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (typically Settings > Privacy > Ads): reset it, delete it, or opt out of ads personalization. When the advertising ID is deleted or you opt out, partners can no longer use it to personalize ads, although you may still see non-personalized ads.

6. Cleartext (HTTP) traffic

Many IPTV and playlist servers only offer unencrypted HTTP. So that you can play such streams, the App allows cleartext (HTTP) connections to the addresses you provide. Data sent over HTTP is not encrypted in transit and could be observed by others on the network. Prefer HTTPS playlists and streams when they are available.
