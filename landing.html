FAQ
Does the app come with channels or streams?

No. Live TV: M3U Player is a player only. It does not provide, host, or distribute any media content. You add your own playlists.

What formats does it support?

M3U and M3U8 playlists. The player handles HLS, MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, AAC, MP3, FLAC and more.

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no accounts. Your data stays on your device.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by ads. There are no in-app purchases.

Why does it allow HTTP (cleartext) traffic?

Many IPTV streams are served over HTTP. The app allows cleartext connections so it can play those streams. Your playlist data itself is stored locally.
